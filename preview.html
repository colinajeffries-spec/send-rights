Get your MP in the room.

1Find your MP

Or search by constituency or MP name

    2Who are you writing as?

    Pick the one closest to you. It sets how your email opens.

    MPs represent everyone in their area, whatever their age, and they want to hear from young people. If you are under 18, it is a good idea to show a parent, carer or teacher your email before you send it.

    Write as a constituent, from your own email address, not on behalf of your school or employer.

    3What worries you most?

    We have ticked the ones people in your position raise most. Change them to suit you. Two or three strong points land better than ten.

    4Add your details

    MPs only act for people who live in their constituency. Your full address shows them you do. Nothing you type here is saved or sent anywhere by this page.

    Did they reply?

    5Send your email

    Read it through and change anything you like. It is your email.

    Open in my email app

    Find your MP's email address on their Parliament profile. Or paste your email into WriteToThem, which sends it for you using your postcode.

    6Ask someone else to do it too

    Save this card and post it in one group you are already in. The web address is on the card.

    On a phone, press and hold the card to save it. On a computer, right click and save the image.

    One email. Five minutes.
