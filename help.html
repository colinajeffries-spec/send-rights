Answer a few questions. We'll show you where you stand in law, the deadlines that matter, what to do next and who can help. It's free and nothing you click is saved.

This is general information about the law in England, not legal advice about your child. It reflects the law as it stands today. The Government has proposed changes, but nothing has changed yet and Education, Health and Care Plans (EHCPs) work exactly as described here. If you're in Wales, Scotland or Northern Ireland the law is different.
