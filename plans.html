      Why can't families see these plans?

      1. No rule says councils have to publish them. The Government left it to each area.
      2. No public consultation was required. Plans were signed off by officials and partnership boards. In many areas councillors saw them only after they had gone to the Government, or not at all.
      3. The Government says more than 9 in 10 plans are approved, but has not said which ones.
      4. A council's past SEND deficit is 90% written off only if the Government approves its plan. The Government's scoring rewards plans with little or no new special school places.

      Ordinary council and Freedom of Information rules still apply. Anyone can ask for their council's plan. Step 1 gives you a ready-made request.

      How we did this

      Between 21 and 22 October 2026 we searched every council's website, Local Offer and committee papers, and news coverage, for its Local SEND Reform Plan. "Nothing public found" means we could not find it online, not that the council has no plan. Figures are as the councils state them. Plans can change after they are reviewed by the Government.
